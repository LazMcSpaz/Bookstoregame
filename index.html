How to play

  • The shop has two modes. Tap Build to add, move and change furniture. Tap Done to go back to running the shop.
  • In Build: pick a tab and tap a piece to add it, or drag it out to where you want it. Drag any piece to move it. Tap it for its menu, tap it again to turn it. Drag it down onto the catalog to sell it.
  • Registers, coffee machines, cakes and small decor go on top of counters and tables. Windows and pictures go on the walls, in half steps, so one can hang centred over a sofa. Half walls go on the lines between tiles.
  • Tap a member of staff to set their jobs: Cash register, Restocking, Food. Tap a job to put it first: the numbers show the order. They do the first job that has work, and the next when it has none.
  • The shop stands still while you build. You can put furniture where people stand: they step aside.
  • Each customer wants a genre. Order books, coffee and pastries from the Stock button. They arrive in your back room.
  • Tap any member of staff, then Change look, to dress them.
  • Staff fetch stock through the back room door and carry it out in a box. Keep the floor in front of the door clear.
  • Rooms tab: tap a floor or wall colour, then tap or drag where it goes: tile by tile, a section of wall at a time, one wall, a whole room, or Whole shop. The back room door can be dragged to any outside wall.
  • In Build, the round marker beside a register, coffee machine or pastry case shows where the server stands. Keep it clear.
  • Decor adds coziness, which brings more customers. New furniture styles join the same categories as they unlock, with the style's name on each card, one style per shop level from level 2: Mid-century (walnut, mustard, teal), Modern (white, light oak, black metal), Cottage (painted wood, florals, wicker), Old Library (dark oak, green, leather, brass) and Cabin (logs, stone, plaid, quilts). The music follows whichever style fills most of the shop.
  • Once you are set up, the shop keeps hours: 9 in the morning to 6 in the evening. At closing you see the day's sales and what customers said. Good reviews bring more customers. The clock stops while you build.
  • New patio in the Rooms tab adds an open-air room with a low fence. Join it to the shop with an archway, furnish it like any room, and pick the fence and an outdoor floor in the Rooms tab.
  • Tap Outside to see the shop from the street. Picture windows, awnings, flower boxes and signs go on the front, and the Rooms tab has finishes for the outside walls. Name your shop in this menu and every sign shows it.
  • Every shop needs a cat. One may wander in after a few days. Tap it to give it a scratch.
  • Children come with their grown-ups once you have Kids pieces. They play while the grown-up shops, and grown-ups with children often want Kids books.
  • Tap the goal at the bottom of the screen to see every goal and its reward.
  • Your shop saves itself as you play. Save this shop keeps a copy under Your shops, where you can load it later. New game keeps the current shop there too, then starts fresh.

Furniture models: KayKit by Kay Lousberg and Tiny Treats by Isa Lousberg, both public domain (CC0).
